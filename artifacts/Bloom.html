Bloom’s taxonomy color key:

  • Remember
  • Understand
  • Apply
  • Analyze
  • Evaluate
  • Create

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize manipulation. Students will identify common techniques used to influence belief and behavior, including emotional appeals, misleading images, selective data, repetition, false authority, and outrage.
  2. Read persuasive messages closely. Students will analyze how language, images, numbers, layout, genre, and platform design shape meaning and audience response.
  3. Question evidence. Students will evaluate sources by asking what is known, what is missing, what is being assumed, who benefits, and what additional context is needed.
  4. Reflect on their own role as interpreters. Students will explain how bias, emotion, identity, convenience, and social pressure can affect what they believe and share.
  5. Use AI as a tool, not an authority. Students will use AI tools to assist with inquiry while checking their outputs against credible sources and human judgment.
  6. Communicate responsible knowledge. Students will create a clear, accessible project that teaches others how to interpret information more carefully and act more responsibly in digital life.
