# Teaching Artifacts

This page should present selected artifacts as proof that the course is active, multimodal, accessible, and engaging. Do not present every weekly experiment at equal weight.

## Featured artifacts

### Point Taken
**Use in proposed course:** Week 4, fallacies, omissions, weak reasoning, and fair persuasion.  
**Role:** Instructor-created game / teaching aid.  
**Important framing:** Students play Point Taken to practice identifying fallacies and persuasive moves. They are not expected to create an agentic game like it.  
**Link:** <https://kgsucf.github.io/PointTaken/>  
**Screenshot:** `assets/point_taken.png`

### 1% Tax Visualization
**Use in proposed course:** Week 8, images, numbers, graphs, and selective evidence.  
**Role:** Teaching example of data rhetoric and framing.  
**Pedagogical point:** The same dataset can support different persuasive claims depending on what is emphasized, omitted, or visually framed.  
**Link:** <https://kgsucf.github.io/ENG6813VisualAnalysis/tax-data-rhetoric.html>  
**Screenshot:** `assets/tax_visualization.png`

### Lincoln Speeches Explorer
**Use in proposed course:** Digital humanities / public-domain corpus example.  
**Role:** Instructor-created model showing how AI-assisted code can help explore a large public-domain text corpus.  
**Pedagogical point:** Digital tools can extend humanities interpretation by increasing scale, but they do not replace human judgment.  
**Link:** <https://kgsucf.github.io/ENG6813AIforCodeandDigitalHumanities/>  
**Screenshot:** `assets/lincoln_explorer.png`

### AI Policy Cartoon
**Use in proposed course:** AI Policy & Access section.  
**Role:** Accessible visual explanation of course AI expectations.  
**Pedagogical point:** Course policies can be clear, non-punitive, and student-facing.  
**Link:** `artifacts/ai-policy-cartoon.html` (embedded live on the AI Policy page); original Claude artifact: <https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/d1bc1f84-4806-4940-a5e2-b578754683ed>  
**Screenshot:** not required — the artifact renders live from the repository.  
**Must include prominently:** Yes.
