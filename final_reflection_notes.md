# Final Reflection Notes

The final course assignment asks for a 750–1000 word reflection and a GitHub Pages portfolio link.

The reflection should include:

1. **Portfolio link**
   - Explain that the e-portfolio presents the current version of ENG 2011: Media Literacy in the Humanities.
   - Mention the portfolio includes the syllabus, teaching statement, signature assignment, AI policy, and selected teaching artifacts.

2. **Connection to at least three readings**
   Possible readings/authors to cite:
   - Ethan Mollick / Mollick and Mollick on assigning AI and keeping the human in the loop
   - Langmead and Vee on pervasive digitality and active learning in digital humanities pedagogy
   - McGinn and Coats on born-pedagogical digital humanities and gradual skill acquisition
   - Brandon Locke on digital/data/computational literacies
   - Drucker on data as capta / visualization as interpretation
   - Johnson and Salter on playful pedagogy and Twine
   - Wiggins and McTighe on backward design
   - Wineburg/McGrew on lateral reading and source evaluation

3. **Experience with AI**
   Possible themes:
   - AI shifted from novelty/tool to collaborator/coach/critic.
   - The main challenge became not whether AI could produce material, but how much to delegate.
   - The course design increasingly centers student judgment, not tool performance.
   - AI is most useful when students have already developed enough understanding to question it.
   - Claude Code and Artifacts helped make playful, interactive, accessible teaching materials possible.
   - Public-domain/openly usable materials became essential because copyright and tool-upload issues are pedagogical, not just legal.

4. **Process reflection**
   - The final portfolio was built through agentic AI workflow but guided by a curated brief.
   - The site demonstrates a course design arc rather than archiving every weekly submission.
   - The strongest learning came from revision: syllabus updates, AI policy revisions, peer-feedback timeline adjustments, and artifact selection.

Do not write the final reflection until the portfolio link exists or is close to complete.
